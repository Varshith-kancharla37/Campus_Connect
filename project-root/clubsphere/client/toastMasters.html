ToastMasters Club

The ToastMasters Club, affiliated with Toastmasters International, is a platform to grow as a leader and public speaker. We help students build confidence, improve communication skills, and develop leadership qualities.

What We Do

• Conduct regular Toastmasters meetings with prepared speeches.
• Organize workshops on public speaking and leadership.
• Host inter-college oratorical competitions.
• Provide mentorship for effective communication skills.

Who Can Join

Any student interested in improving public speaking, leadership, or communication skills can join. No prior experience is required.
