TechPost ClubCode

TechPost ClubCode, started on March 29, 2022, is focused on sharing the latest tech trends, coding practices, and building impactful real-time software projects. We aim to launch a skills-based platform for SVECW students to educate and expose young minds to emerging technologies.[](https://svecw.edu.in/student-clubs/)

What We Do

• Conduct workshops on emerging technologies like AI, IoT, and blockchain.
• Organize coding bootcamps and hackathons.
• Publish student articles on tech trends and innovations.
• Develop real-time software projects for practical learning.

Who Can Join

Any student interested in coding, software development, or staying updated with tech trends can join. No prior experience is required—just enthusiasm for technology.
