Rhythmic Thunders

The idea behind establishing the Rhythmic Thunders Dance Club is to promote dance. It was formed to bring out hidden dancing talents and give a platform to students who have passion and love for dance. The club focuses not only on entertainment but also on spreading motivational messages that inspire the student community. Every year, the club welcomes new talented dancers with open hearts, helping them express themselves and showcase their creativity through performances and competitions.

What We Do

• Organize dance workshops across various styles (classical, hip-hop, contemporary, etc.)
• Perform during college events, festivals, and competitions
• Host annual dance battles and inter-department cultural fests
• Promote awareness campaigns and social messages through dance performances

Who Can Join

Any student who is passionate about dance—be it classical, western, freestyle, or fusion—is welcome to join. No formal training required, just a willingness to learn, perform, and express.
