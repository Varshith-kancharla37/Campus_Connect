Painting Club

The Painting Club is for those who love art and creativity. We provide opportunities to learn painting techniques, explore various styles, and exhibit your imagination through colors.

What We Do

• Conduct painting workshops and art classes.
• Organize exhibitions to showcase student artwork.
• Explore various painting styles like watercolor, acrylic, and oil.
• Collaborate on mural projects for campus beautification.

Who Can Join

Any student with an interest in painting or art can join. Beginners and experienced artists are both welcome.
