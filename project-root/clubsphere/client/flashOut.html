Flash It Out

Flash It Out is a media club that captures the essence of campus life through short films, reels, and photography. We aim to tell compelling stories and document memorable moments.

What We Do

• Create short films and reels for campus events.
• Organize photography workshops and contests.
• Document college festivals and activities.
• Collaborate on media projects for social awareness.

Who Can Join

Any student interested in filmmaking, photography, or media production can join. No prior experience is needed—just a passion for storytelling.
