Idea Club

The Idea Club is a creative space for students to express innovative ideas, develop reading habits, and sharpen analytical thinking. We encourage brainstorming sessions, debates, and activities to foster intellectual growth and creativity.

What We Do

• Host brainstorming sessions and idea pitching contests.
• Organize book reading and discussion circles.
• Conduct debates and group discussions on contemporary topics.
• Collaborate on creative projects to enhance analytical skills.

Who Can Join

Any student eager to explore new ideas, improve critical thinking, or engage in intellectual discussions is welcome to join.
