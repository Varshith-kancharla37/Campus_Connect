Eco Club

The Eco Club is dedicated to promoting eco-consciousness through tree plantation drives, waste reduction initiatives, and awareness campaigns. We aim to foster a sustainable campus and community.

What We Do

• Organize tree plantation drives and clean-up campaigns.
• Conduct workshops on waste reduction and recycling.
• Promote eco-friendly practices on campus.
• Host awareness events on environmental issues.

Who Can Join

Any student interested in environmental conservation and sustainability can join. No prior experience is needed—just a passion for protecting our planet.
