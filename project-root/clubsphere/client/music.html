Music Club

The Music Club at SVECW is dedicated to promoting Indian Classical Music and fostering a love for vocal performance and rhythms. We offer training sessions, organize musical events, and provide a platform for students to showcase their talents in various genres.

What We Do

• Conduct workshops on Indian Classical Music and contemporary genres.
• Organize intra-college music competitions and performances.
• Collaborate with local artists for masterclasses and concerts.
• Host jam sessions to encourage creativity and collaboration.

Who Can Join

Any student passionate about music, whether a beginner or experienced, is welcome. Join us to explore vocal techniques, instrumental skills, or simply enjoy music.
